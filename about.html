About CMBverse

CMBverse is an interactive website for learning about the Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB), developed by Chase Enlowsmith under the mentorship of Dr. Gabriele Montefalcone. We designed CMBverse to make learning cosmology more engaging and intuitive, letting students directly visualize the impact of each parameter of the standard cosmological model on the CMB. Here, you can explore a wide range of \( \Lambda\rm{CDM} \) cosmological parameter configurations via our interactive plots and see the resulting power spectra in real time, as predicted by our neural-network emulators of the Einstein–Boltzmann solver CLASS.

In the future, we plan to extend CMBverse to CMB secondaries, the small distortions imprinted on the CMB as its photons interact with matter and structure on their journey to us. The incoming data from the Simons Observatory will measure these secondary signals with unprecedented precision, making them a natural next step for CMBverse. In addition, we aim to bring the same interactive approach to other probes of the large-scale structure of the universe, such as the matter power spectrum and the 21 cm power spectrum from cosmic dawn. Finally, if you are interested in CMBverse and the philosophy behind it, we encourage you to contact us and join the effort.
