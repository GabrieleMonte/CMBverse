About CMBverse

CMBverse is an interactive website for learning about the Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB). Explore a wide range of \( \Lambda\rm{CDM} \) cosmological parameter configurations via our interactive plots, and see the resulting power spectra in real time. Powered by our Einstein-Boltzmann solver emulators, CMBverse delivers accurate visualization at live-rendering speeds. In the future, we plan to extend CMBverse to CMB secondaries and to other cosmological probes, such as the matter power spectrum and the 21 cm power spectrum from cosmic dawn. CMBverse is developed by Chase Enlowsmith under the mentorship of Gabriele Montefalcone.
